# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Nidara project aims to provide a respectful, constructive environment for everyone who contributes, reports an issue, or uses the project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and patience.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions in good faith.
- Give useful, specific feedback without attacking people.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's personal or research data.
- Follow the project's contribution and security reporting guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Unwanted sexual attention or publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberate intimidation, disruption, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Sharing credentials, private records, or other people's research data.
- Retaliation against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents or participates in the Nidara project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately to the maintainer through the contact method listed on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/diegopn). Do not include incident details or personal information in a public issue. If no private contact method is available, open an issue asking how to contact the maintainer and leave out the details.

The maintainer will review reports as promptly as possible and may remove content, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. The response will consider the context, impact, and safety of the people involved. If a concern involves the maintainer, use GitHub's platform reporting options.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
